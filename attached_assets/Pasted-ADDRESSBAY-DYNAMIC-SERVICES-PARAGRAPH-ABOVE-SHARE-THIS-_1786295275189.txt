ADDRESSBAY — DYNAMIC SERVICES PARAGRAPH ABOVE “SHARE THIS PROFILE”

TASK:

Add a dynamic, automatically generated services paragraph immediately ABOVE the existing “Share this profile” tab/button on the company profile page.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT redesign the page.
- Do NOT move or modify the existing “Share this profile” tab/button.
- Do NOT change the company profile layout.
- Do NOT change the existing Overview or company information sections.
- Do NOT hard-code service names or URLs.
- Use the existing service/link configuration or service data available in the application.
- The paragraph must automatically update whenever the available services or their URLs are changed.
- This must work for all countries and all company pages.

==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

The paragraph should naturally connect the company profile with relevant AddressBay services.

It should read like useful contextual navigation, NOT like an advertisement block and NOT like keyword stuffing.

Example:

"Looking for professional business and compliance services? Explore our services for company registration, business compliance, trademark registration and related business needs."

The individual service names should be clickable links.

Example:

"Looking for professional business and compliance services? Explore our
Company Registration,
Trademark Registration,
GST Registration
and other business services."

Each service name must link to its configured service URL.

==================================================
2. DYNAMIC TEMPLATE
==================================================

Use this generic template:

"Looking for services related to {{company_context}}? Explore {{service_links}} and other business services available through AddressBay."

Where:

{{company_context}}

is generated from available company data when appropriate, such as:

- company registration
- business compliance
- company formation
- business services

Do NOT make unsupported claims about what the company actually needs.

{{service_links}}

is dynamically generated from the currently available service links.

==================================================
3. SERVICE LINK GENERATION
==================================================

DO NOT hard-code:

- service names
- URLs
- number of services
- service order

Instead, retrieve services from the existing service configuration/data source.

For example, if the current service configuration contains:

Company Registration → /services/company-registration
Trademark Registration → /services/trademark-registration
GST Registration → /services/gst-registration
LLP Registration → /services/llp-registration

the paragraph should automatically render:

"Looking for business registration and compliance services? Explore Company Registration, Trademark Registration, GST Registration and LLP Registration services available through AddressBay."

Where each service name is a clickable link.

If the service configuration changes to:

Company Registration
Trademark Registration
Business Tax Services

the paragraph must automatically change accordingly without modifying the component/template.

==================================================
4. LINK RULES
==================================================

Every service name shown in the paragraph must use its actual configured URL.

Do NOT construct URLs manually if a service URL already exists in the system.

Do NOT create broken links.

Before rendering a service link, verify:

- service name exists
- URL exists
- URL is valid
- service is active/published
- service is intended for public users

Inactive/unpublished services must not appear.

==================================================
5. NUMBER OF SERVICES
==================================================

Do not display an unnecessarily long list.

Use the configured priority/order of services.

Recommended:

1–3 services:
show all naturally.

4+ services:
show the highest-priority/relevant services and then use wording such as:

"Explore Company Registration, Trademark Registration, GST Registration and other business services available through AddressBay."

The exact number can be controlled by the existing service configuration.

Do not hard-code a fixed list into the component.

==================================================
6. GENERIC COUNTRY SUPPORT
==================================================

This must work for:

India
United States
United Kingdom
Australia
Singapore
and future countries.

Do NOT write:

"Indian companies can..."

unless the company/country data actually indicates India.

Do NOT hard-code Indian services into the template.

The same component must work with country-specific services.

Example:

India company:
"Explore company registration, GST registration, trademark registration and other business services..."

UK company:
"Explore company formation, trademark registration and other business services..."

US company:
"Explore business registration, trademark registration and other business services..."

These services must come from the service configuration/data, not from hard-coded country logic.

==================================================
7. COMPANY CONTEXT
==================================================

The paragraph may use company information where useful:

{{company_name}}
{{country}}
{{entity_type}}
{{city}}
{{industry}}

But avoid excessive repetition.

For example, do NOT generate:

"Infosys Limited is a company and Infosys Limited can use company services..."

Instead use natural contextual language.

==================================================
8. MISSING SERVICES

If there are no active service links available:

DO NOT render an empty paragraph.

DO NOT show:

"Explore undefined..."

"Explore null..."

"Explore N/A..."

Simply hide this new section.

==================================================
9. DUPLICATE LINK PROTECTION

Do not display the same service twice.

Normalize services by service ID or canonical URL.

If duplicate service records exist, show only one.

==================================================
10. PLACEMENT

The page should become:

Company Profile
↓
Company information / existing content
↓
Existing sections
↓
[ DYNAMIC SERVICES PARAGRAPH ]
↓
Share this profile
↓
Everything else

The existing “Share this profile” component must remain exactly where it currently is.

Only insert the new paragraph immediately above it.

==================================================
11. DESIGN

Keep this visually subtle.

Do NOT create a large card.

Do NOT create a new hero section.

Use the existing AddressBay typography, spacing and responsive styles.

The paragraph should look like a natural part of the company profile.

Service names should have the existing AddressBay link treatment.

Example:

Looking for business and compliance services? Explore
Company Registration,
Trademark Registration,
GST Registration
and other business services available through AddressBay.

==================================================
12. SEO REQUIREMENTS

The paragraph should provide useful internal navigation.

Use descriptive anchor text.

GOOD:
Company Registration
Trademark Registration
GST Registration

BAD:
Click here
Learn more
Visit this
Service

Do NOT keyword stuff.

Do NOT generate dozens of links.

Do NOT repeat the same service links elsewhere unnecessarily.

The content should be genuinely useful to visitors.

==================================================
13. ARCHITECTURE

Create a reusable component/helper, for example:

DynamicServicesParagraph

or

CompanyServicesLinks

The component should receive the company context and available services from the existing data layer.

Do not duplicate service data inside the component.

Recommended conceptual structure:

Company Page
    ↓
Company Data
    ↓
Active Service Configuration
    ↓
DynamicServicesParagraph
    ↓
ShareProfile

The service configuration should remain the single source of truth.

==================================================
14. AUTOMATIC UPDATES

This is critical.

If an administrator:

- adds a new service
- removes a service
- changes a service URL
- changes a service name
- disables a service
- changes service priority
- changes country availability

the company profile paragraph must automatically reflect the new configuration.

No manual editing of individual company pages should be required.

Do NOT store the generated paragraph as static text in every company record.

Generate it at render time from the current service data/configuration.

==================================================
15. FINAL TEST

Test with:

1. Company having 2 active services.
2. Company having 5+ active services.
3. Company with no active services.
4. Service URL changed.
5. Service disabled.
6. New service added.
7. Duplicate service exists.
8. India company.
9. US company.
10. UK company.
11. Singapore company.
12. Australia company if available.

Verify:

- Paragraph appears immediately above Share this profile.
- Existing Share this profile is unchanged.
- Service names are clickable.
- URLs come from the service configuration.
- No hard-coded service URLs.
- No undefined/null text.
- No duplicate services.
- Disabled services disappear automatically.
- Newly enabled services appear automatically.
- Existing page design remains unchanged.
- Mobile layout remains unchanged.
- Desktop layout remains unchanged.

IMPORTANT:
This task ONLY adds the dynamic services paragraph above the existing “Share this profile” component.

DO NOT make unrelated UI, database, SEO, routing, or page-layout changes.
